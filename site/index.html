The work

You are the maker, then the one who stays.

Play as Elyon and shape the first garden: light, waters, land, the two trees, the lights of the sky, the creatures, and Adam and Eve. Walk among them, name what lives, speak or keep silent, and see which ending the garden keeps. This is an original telling, not a scripture.

Eight days of creation

Light, waters, dry land, the grove, the luminaries, the living creatures, humanity, and a rest that will not be skipped.

Sky View / Walk Among

Look down on the work, or press into the grass. The gold action follows you from the sky to the ground.

The garden

Water, meadow, bloom, and the two trees. Breath is spent when you shape what is already there.

Adam and Eve

Form them, speak with them, and draw near on the rest day. They answer from the garden they are standing in.

Four endings

Harmony, exile, wonder, or order. The garden kept, the eastern grass, the open orchard, or the closed question.

Voice chat

Address them in text. Voice speaks and listens when the window can, and the reply remains when it cannot.

Elyon

A mythic simulator of the first garden.

Elyon is a single-player mythic simulator. You shape eight days of creation, then walk among Adam and Eve. Sky View and Walk Among share one garden. Four endings remain: harmony, exile, wonder, and order. Text chat always works. Voice uses the window when it can.

The short line, the long line, and the fact sheet are in the kit.
